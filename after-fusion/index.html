FUSION CONTROL ROOM / STARTER KIT

After fusion

A reconstructed target, a prescribed plasma, recorded neutron tracks, and a calculated thermal response. Explore each model at your own pace.

Prepared model playback. This browser does not run a solver. The plasma and neutron source are separate examples. Transport deposition and thermal response are coupled; this is not validated reactor performance.

The poster and explanations remain available without WebGL.

Reconstruction · NOT A SIMULATION

Target geometry is a literature-based reconstruction. No target-specific implosion was calculated.

How to read this example
  • Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and right-drag to pan. Nothing plays automatically.
  • Plasma colors show temperature; opacity is a density display function, not a radiation-transfer image.
  • Transport shows a direction-selected subset of recorded neutron and photon histories. Heating uses all 64,000 histories.
  • Wall and coolant colors have separate normalization. Equal colors do not mean equal temperature rises.
  • The heat removed fraction is deposited energy sent to a sink, not electrical efficiency.
