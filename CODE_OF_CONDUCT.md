# Community conduct

Be welcoming to beginners, precise about evidence, and considerate when
disagreeing. Critique calculations and assumptions rather than people. Give
credit for ideas, code, and data. Do not publish private personal information,
harass contributors, or make discriminatory remarks.

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact details at
https://mortonlabs.ai/contact, with the relevant context. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or limit participation
to keep discussion constructive. Reports are handled with discretion.
